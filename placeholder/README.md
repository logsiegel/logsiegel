# Logsiegel

**Prove your AI didn't lie.**

Logsiegel gives AI systems and agents a tamper-evident action trail — every step
hash-chained and signed, verifiable offline by anyone you hand a receipt to.
Open source, no blockchain, runs where your agent runs.

This is a **name-reservation pre-release**. The SDK (hash-chained log, standalone
receipts, RFC 6962-style proofs, crypto-shredding, LiteLLM adapter) ships with
early access.

- Early access: https://logsiegel.trialcode.net
